The short version

  • No account, no name, no email, no password. You can play the whole game without giving us anything.
  • The puzzles you send to friends are stored on our server as physics moves (no text, photos or names).
  • We use anonymous analytics to improve the game, and ads to fund it. You can remove ads with a purchase.
  • You can delete your online data inside the app: Settings → Privacy & support → Delete my online data.

We do not sell your data. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or messages.

Sharing

When you send a challenge, the link or code you share lets the recipient see the puzzle and its twists. Do not send a link to anyone you don’t want to play it. Service providers listed above process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Tracking: choose “Ask App Not to Track” on the prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete online data: in the app (see above). This permanently deletes your anonymous ID and your counters. Puzzles that other players built on top of yours remain, but are no longer linked to you.
  • Remove ads: available as an in-app purchase. Restore purchases is in the Shop.
  • Local data is removed by Settings → Reset, or by deleting the app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Ads are configured for a general audience.

Retention and security

Puzzles and counters are kept until you delete them or the game is shut down. Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
