How to play

  1. Drag planks from the tray into the puzzle and tilt them so the blue ball reaches the gold ring.
  2. Press GO. Retry as often as you like.
  3. When you solve it, change one rule and send the new puzzle to a friend. They solve it, twist it again, and pass it on.
  4. Open My chains to replay how a puzzle evolved and share it as a GIF.

Common questions

A friend’s link doesn’t open the game. Install RULESHIFT first, then tap the link again, or copy the message and paste it into the app’s “Paste a challenge” box.

I got a message saying a challenge can’t be solved or is damaged. The link or code was probably cut off. Ask your friend to send it again.

I reinstalled / got a new phone. Open Shop → Restore purchases to get “Remove ads” back. Progress is stored on your device.

Delete my data. Settings → Privacy & support → Delete my online data.
