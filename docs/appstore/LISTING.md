## Description
Every RULESHIFT puzzle takes about 20 seconds. Drag a few planks, press GO, get the ball to the gold ring.

Then comes the twist: change ONE rule and send the puzzle to a friend.
Gravity flips. The ball grows. A crosswind blows. The whole room mirrors. Your friend has to solve YOUR version, then twist it again and pass it on. After a few hops nobody recognises the original, and you can watch the whole chain replay and share it as a GIF.

- 24 handcrafted puzzles and a brand-new Daily Puzzle for everyone, every day
- 10 rule twists, and every twist is verified solvable before it is sent
- Send challenges with a link. No account, no sign-up
- Chains that branch: several friends can continue the same puzzle
- Replay how a puzzle evolved and share it as a GIF
- Instant retry, juicy feedback, tiny sessions
- Daily streaks with freezes so one missed day doesn't hurt

Playing, twisting and sending are always free. Optional rewarded videos give you hints; "Remove ads" is a one-time purchase.
