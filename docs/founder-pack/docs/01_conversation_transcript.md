Once you solve the puzzle, you get to change one rule before sending it to another player.

Why I think this has viral potential
Think about what happens naturally:

Someone sends their friend a puzzle they modified.

Their friend tries to beat it.

Their friend changes the rules and sends it to somebody else.

Five people later, the original puzzle is almost unrecognizable.

Everyone can watch the transformation.

1. Challenge videos

2. Funny videos

3. Competitive videos

"I managed to solve a level that 12 other people failed."

That's the marketing mechanism I want built into the actual gameplay.
