# Alternative concepts considered

## Round 1 (original five)
| Concept | Idea | Dev difficulty | Revenue potential (session's own estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Second Self | Cooperate with ghosts of your own past runs to solve puzzle rooms | Medium | High |
| The Last Alibi | Daily mystery in a miniature city; build a case from contradictions | High | High |
| Worlds Within | Tiny civilizations inside everyday objects | Very high | High |
| Signal Lost | Decode alien transmissions via sound/symbol puzzles | Medium | Medium-High |
| After Hours | Run a strange 24-hour night business with story events | High | High |

These are development assessments, not forecasts. Second Self was the first-round favorite: easy to explain in a short video, no expensive 3D, many levels.

## Round 2 (viral-focused)
- **A. RULESHIFT (chosen):** pass-along rule-changing puzzles. Strength: built-in sharing and UGC.
- **B. The World Is Watching:** one shared evolving world influenced by thousands of players. Strength: community and return reasons. Needs a large player base to feel alive.
- **C. What Happens Next?:** predict outcomes of bizarre physics situations. Strength: shareable reaction clips.
