# Launch and marketing

## Staged build (from the earlier session)
1. Core gameplay: physics puzzle, rule system, level generator. Must be genuinely fun first.
2. Social sharing: challenge links, chains, friend challenges, auto replay videos.
3. Retention: daily puzzle, streaks, achievements, progression, leaderboards.
4. Monetization: ads, purchases, subscriptions, analytics.
5. Launch: test with real users, short-form videos, measure retention, iterate.

## The most important assumption
Do people actually send challenges to friends? Test this before a full launch. Metrics: send rate per solve, chain depth, chain completion, D1/D7 retention.

## Marketing mechanisms
- Built-in shareable replay of a chain's evolution.
- Challenge videos, funny evolution videos, competitive clips for TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- Daily puzzle for habit and shared conversation.

## App Store notes
- Apps with user-generated content need reporting, blocking, and moderation.
- Copycat apps are rejected; submit a complete, working app with functional purchases.
- Prepare privacy policy, ATT handling, screenshots, preview video.

## Platform decision
Open question from the session: iPhone first, Android later (assumed in PLAN.md) vs simultaneous.

## Optional
Weekly research report on new hits, download trends, and monetization lessons to spot saturation early.
