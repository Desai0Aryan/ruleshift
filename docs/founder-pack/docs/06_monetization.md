# Monetization plan

All prices are proposed test prices, not validated optima.

| Source | Example |
|---|---|
| Rewarded ads | Extra hint or optional retry |
| Interstitial ads | Occasional, between sessions, never mid-puzzle |
| Remove Ads | $4.99 one-time |
| Premium membership | $4.99/month |
| Cosmetic packs | $0.99 - $9.99 |
| Special puzzle packs | $2.99 - $5.99 |

## Membership ideas
Exclusive environments, extra creator tools, premium visual effects, additional daily challenges, advanced stats.

## Principles
- Keep the core challenge/multiplayer experience free so the social loop grows.
- Monetize around the experience, not participation.
- Retention matters more than short-term ad impressions.

## Order of work
Do not integrate monetization until the Phase 2 gate (people actually send challenges) passes. Then add RevenueCat, AdMob, and PostHog, and A/B test ad frequency and prices.

## Cautions
- Ads and IAP revenue at launch is not guaranteed.
- If the app is aimed at or likely used by children, ad and data rules (COPPA, Apple Kids rules) change significantly.
