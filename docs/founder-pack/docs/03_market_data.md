## Qualitative takeaways cited
- Hybrid puzzle games (simple play plus progression, collections, monetization) perform strongly.
- Block and sort puzzles showed substantial revenue growth.
- Hybrid monetization (ads plus IAP) is increasingly standard.
- Apple requires working purchases and complete submissions, and rejects indistinguishable copycat apps.

## Interpretation (from the session)
1. Puzzle has large commercial demand.
2. Simple gameplay plus deeper progression monetizes well.
3. A genuinely new puzzle mechanic may beat entering a crowded genre.

## Caveat
High genre revenue is concentrated in a few top titles. Genre size does not predict revenue for a new entrant.
